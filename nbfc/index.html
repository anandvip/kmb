The premise

Two bad options, and a third nobody mentions

Lock your money in a Fixed Deposit and watch inflation quietly eat it — or throw it at the stock market and hope.

For decades, that's been the entire menu handed to young Indian investors. This book argues there's a third arena, one where institutional money already parks its cash — hiding in plain sight inside every Demat account most people already have open.

It starts with defense: learning to strip the marketing off a shiny new fintech app, cross-check it against the RBI's Master Registry, and recognize that an 18% "guaranteed" return is a warning label, not a bargain.

⚠ 18% guaranteed returns are the tell, not the pitch

Then it turns to offense. Chapter 3 is where the book walks into the RBI's Upper and Middle Layer institutions — the titans that don't just lend money, they borrow it — and shows exactly how a retail investor lends to them directly.

The regulatory ladder

Where the apex begins

The RBI sorts India's 9,075 registered NBFCs into layers by systemic weight. Most of the noise — and most of the risk — sits at the bottom. Chapter 3 hunts at the top.
